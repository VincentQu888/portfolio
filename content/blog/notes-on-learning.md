title: "Notes on learning in public"
date: "2026-07-10"
summary: "A few things that have helped me learn faster."
---

I learn best when I have something real to point at. A tutorial I finished a
week ago fades fast; a project I shipped sticks around.

A few habits that have helped:

1. **Build the smallest thing that works, then grow it.** A working toy beats a
   perfect plan.
2. **Write down what confused you.** Future-me is always grateful.
3. **Read the source.** Docs tell you *what*; source tells you *why*.

> The best way to learn a system is to try to change it.

That's the spirit behind this site — a place to change things and see what
happens.
